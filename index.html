RATA / PART 75

Part 75 RATA — Paired Run Data

PART 75 — REQUIRED FIELD REMINDER: Perform the required post-run system bias check after every run. Do not use calibration-gas dilution devices for Part 75 reference-method RATA work.

Enter paired reference-method and CEMS values. Select the monitoring system and the app will evaluate every applicable Part 75 acceptance pathway, the earned RATA frequency, and the separate statistical bias test.

SO₂: enter concentration in ppm. Low-emitter alternate limits require an RM mean ≤ 250 ppm.

Leave off when entering reference-method values already corrected outside the app. When enabled, the corrected value—not the raw average—is used in the RATA statistics. This is separate from the Part 75 CEMS statistical bias test and BAF below.
